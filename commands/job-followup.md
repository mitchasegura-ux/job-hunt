# /job-followup - Draft follow-ups on quiet applications

Invoke the `job-hunting` skill and follow its **Follow up** section.

`$ARGUMENTS` may name a company or set a threshold like `3 weeks`. Default is
anything applied more than 14 days ago with no employer response.

For each, draft four or five sentences: the role and roughly when you applied,
one concrete new thing if there is one, restated interest, a close. Their voice,
no em-dashes.

Never send anything. Draft, show, let the candidate send it.

Say which applications you are deliberately not following up on and why, for
example a posting that has since closed or a role they have cooled on.
